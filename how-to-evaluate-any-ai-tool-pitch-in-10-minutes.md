Every vendor call this year has an AI angle. Most of the pitches sound similar, so I've settled on a short checklist that separates the useful ones from the noise quickly.

## Ask what happens when it's wrong

If a vendor can't clearly describe their failure mode, and who's accountable when the model gets something wrong, that's the answer. Confident demos hide this by design.

## Ask where your data goes

Not as a compliance exercise, as a product question. If they can't explain data flow in one sentence, the product wasn't built with that question in mind.

## Ask for the boring use case, not the demo one

Demos are optimized for the moment. Ask instead: "walk me through the least exciting task your customers use this for every day." That's where you learn if it's actually load-bearing.

## The takeaway

None of this requires deep technical knowledge, it requires being willing to ask a plain question and sit with an uncomfortable non-answer.
